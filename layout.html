Tangled

Tangled is a 2010 animated film by Walt Disney Animation Studios, inspired by the fairy tale Rapunzel by the Brothers Grimm. It follows a young princess with magical golden hair who is locked in a tower by Mother Gothel. She escapes with the help of a charming thief and discovers her true identity.
Directed by Nathan Greno and Byron Howard, and written by Dan Fogelman, it stars Mandy Moore, Zachary Levi, and Donna Murphy. The film tells the story of Rapunzel, a young princess with magical long blonde hair who tries to leave her secret tower. She accepts the aid of an intruder, the outlaw Flynn Rider, to take her out into the world which she has never seen.

Main Characters

Rapunzel(main female character). She is the spirited, independent heroine of Disney's 2010 animated film Tangled, voiced by Mandy Moore. She is a princess with 70 feet of magical, golden hair, kept in a tower by Mother Gothel, who escapes with the help of thief Flynn Rider to see the world's floating lanterns.

Flynn Rider(main male character). His real name is Eugene Fitzherbert. Voiced by Zachary Levi, he begins the film as a charming, wanted thief who reluctantly helps Rapunzel escape her tower, eventually falling in love with her and revealing his true, caring self.
